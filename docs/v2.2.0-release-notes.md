# ZYLO v2.2.0 — Release Notes 🎬

Next-Generation, Ad-Free Streaming & Media Hub for Android & Web.

### 🌟 What's New in v2.2.0:
- **Verified Theatrical & OTT Availability System**:
  - Separate tracking for Theatrical vs OTT release dates.
  - Theatrical-only movies display countdowns and Remind Me buttons, preventing broken playback.
  - Dual countdown timers for both theatrical premieres and official digital availability.
- **20 Integrated Streaming Servers & Resolvers**:
  - Complete integration of all Nxsha servers (Nitro [Multi-Lang], Mahalala [Multi-Lang], Multi-bill, AwsPly, CastVid, StremFx, Citadel, PureWave, Vixo, Prvibd, Ophm, VidStpM, ShowNitro, VidHindi, VidEmd, VidEmt, VidFk, VidMv, VidPro, VidBo).
- **Website Playback Modernization**:
  - Direct HTML5 / HLS.js streaming with automatic embed container fallback on `https://zyloweb.vercel.app`.
- **Advanced Player Controls**:
  - **100-Second Forward Buffering**: Deep buffering for bufferless playback.
  - **Quality Chooser**: Auto, 4K, 1080p, 720p, 480p, 360p selection.
  - **Default Audio Language Selection**: Automatic preference for Hindi/South/English audio tracks.
  - **Jump to Time**: Accurate timestamp navigation (HH:MM:SS or MM:SS).
  - **In-Player Episode Side Panel**: Browse seasons and episodes directly inside the player.
  - **Seek-Bar Preview**: Visual thumbnail preview while seeking.
- **Regional Indian Catalogs**:
  - Replaced collection folder placeholders with live, dynamic movie rows for Bollywood, South Indian Cinema, and Punjabi Movies.
- **Intelligent Featured Banner & Personalization**:
  - Curated mix of major Hollywood and Indian cinema releases.
  - Profile-specific recommendations based on viewing history.
- **Continue Watching Enhancements**:
  - 3-dot action sheet and quick cross button with Undo for removing titles.
- **Safe Area & Display Cutout Protection**:
  - Dialogs, toasts, and popups respect waterdrop notches, punch-hole cameras, and navigation bars.
- **Seamless AniDash Integration**:
  - Coordinated launch of AniDash (`com.anidash.app`) with deep-linked anime and episode mapping, plus official install fallback.
- **Watch Together**:
  - Synchronized real-time playback with chat on `https://zyloweb.vercel.app/party/{code}`.
